User Guide & Feature Manual

⏱️ Timestamp Resume

LocalTube automatically remembers your exact playback position for every video. Red progress bars on thumbnails show how far you've watched, and clicking a video prompts you to resume where you left off.

▶️ Up Next Auto-Play

When enabled in the player, clicking a video or reaching the end of playback will automatically stream the next video in your active queue or filter list.

🏷️ Custom Tagging

Add custom tags to any video by clicking the tag badge. Tags appear as filter chips at the top of your library for instant one-click filtering.

📜 Watch History

Chronological logging of every video you watch. View your watch history anytime from the sidebar and clear it with a single click.

📁 Folder Memory (IndexedDB)

Directory handles are securely remembered using browser IndexedDB. With 1-click on the reconnect banner, you instantly restore access to your video folders without re-selecting.
